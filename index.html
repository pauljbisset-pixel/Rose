The Artist

Coastal magic,
layer by layer

I am an artist based in Instow, where the Devon coastline's wild beauty inspires everything I create. My love for creativity started with cake design and textiles, but in the last six years painting has become my true passion.

I love collecting seaweed from the beach to work into my paintings — it adds a special texture and tells a story of the sea.

I work mainly with oils, mixing in wax to build up layers and create depth. Bright, vivid colours are my thing; I want my paintings to capture the energy and spirit of the coast.

I hope my paintings bring a bit of coastal magic into your world.

Every piece begins with a walk on the beach.
Rose Budge
